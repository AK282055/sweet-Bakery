Privacy Policy

At Sweet Crumbs Bakehouse ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while placing cake orders, browsing our website, or contacting our concierge.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account, we collect: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, recipient details, custom celebration card messages, and payment method details. Payment transactions are processed directly by certified RBI/PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To bake, personalize, package, and deliver your cakes to the exact destination.
  • To dispatch order tracking links, invoice receipts, and delivery slot notifications via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
  • To notify you about seasonal festive offers, discounts, and menu launches (you may unsubscribe at any time).

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption to safeguard all transmitted sensitive data. We never sell, lease, or distribute your personal contact information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Contact Privacy Officer

If you have questions about our privacy practices or wish to delete your customer records, please email our team at privacy@sweetcrumbsbakery.com.
