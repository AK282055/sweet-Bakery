Logistics & Care

Shipping & Delivery Policy

To ensure your celebration cakes arrive in pristine, chilled, picture-perfect condition, we manage specialized temperature-controlled courier vans and sanitized delivery carriers.

1. Delivery Zones & Timings

We deliver across city limits with standard slots: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM, 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM, and 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM. We also offer special Midnight Surprise deliveries (11:00 PM – 12:00 AM).

2. Shipping Charges

  • Standard Delivery: FREE on all orders above ₹799 (nominal ₹49 for orders below ₹799).
  • Fixed 1-Hour Precision Slot: ₹75 priority routing charge.
  • Midnight Surprise Delivery: ₹149 night logistics fee.

3. Protective Packaging

Every cake is secured in a heavy-duty, 5-ply corrugated insulated cake box with anti-skid baseboards and food-grade dry ice packs.
