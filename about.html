Our Bakehouse Story

Baking Memories Into Every Slice Since 2018

Sweet Crumbs started with a humble stand mixer and an unyielding commitment: never cut corners on ingredients, never use premixes, and treat every cake as the sweet centerpiece of someone's happiest day.

Pure Artisan Philosophy

From Our Ovens Directly To Your Festivities

In a world of mass-manufactured, frozen cake sponges, Sweet Crumbs stands apart. Every morning at 4:30 AM, our pastry chefs fire up our European deck ovens. We crack real farm eggs (and whip natural plant-based substitutes for our 100% eggless patrons), melt genuine Belgian couverture chocolate, and whip fresh farm dairy cream.

Whether it's an intimate midnight anniversary surprise or a 5-kilogram grand wedding tiered cake, each masterpiece is piped by hand with artistic finesse and chilled to perfection.
