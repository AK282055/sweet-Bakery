Cancellation & Refund Policy

We strive for total customer delight in every celebration. Because our products are perishable foods freshly baked to individual specifications, our cancellation and refund terms are structured as follows:

1. Order Cancellation Windows

  • Standard Orders: Cancellations made at least 4 hours before the start of the scheduled delivery slot will receive a 100% full refund to the original payment source.
  • Baking In-Progress: Once our chef team has baked the sponge and commenced frosting/piping decoration, cancellations cannot be processed.
  • Custom Fondant / Wedding Tiered Cakes: Require at least 24 hours prior notice for cancellation.

2. Transit Damage / Quality Concerns

In the rare event that your cake arrives structurally damaged, toppled during transit, or incorrect flavor:

  • Please take 2 clear photographs of the cake in its packaging upon delivery.
  • WhatsApp the photos along with your Order ID to +91 98765 43210 or email orders@sweetcrumbsbakery.com within 2 hours of receipt.
  • We will immediately dispatch an emergency fresh replacement or issue an instant 100% refund.

3. Refund Processing Speed

Approved refunds are credited back to the original UPI / Card / Net Banking account within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank's settlement cycle.
